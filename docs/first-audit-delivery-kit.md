## One-page offer

**Find the useful first step for AI in your business.**

We review up to three recurring tasks and give you a clear plan for what to improve, what it could be worth, and what to leave alone.

**Pilot fee:** From CAD $500 plus applicable tax during the pilot, subject to agreed scope. Standard price to test afterward: $750. No required subscription or follow-on purchase.

**Includes:** a 60-minute interview, review of agreed redacted examples, a short prioritized action plan, one worked example, a 30-minute readout and one clarification round. Target delivery: five business days after receiving the agreed inputs.

**Your commitment:** one decision-maker, access to someone who does the work, and two or three non-sensitive examples. You own the delivered plan.

**Separate scope:** software implementation, software fees, ongoing support and grant applications. Funding eligibility and savings are not guaranteed. Any funded project needs its own eligibility and approval process before work starts.

**Next step:** a free 15-minute fit call. A written service agreement sets the scope, payment terms and delivery dates before work begins.

## Interview sequence

1. Ask them to walk through one recent task from start to finish.
2. Observe where information arrives, is copied, is checked and is handed off.
3. Record actual frequency and effort; identify estimates as estimates.
4. Ask about exceptions, mistakes, customer consent and sensitive information.
5. Check what their existing tools already support before suggesting another tool.
6. Agree what success would look like and who will measure it.

## Fictional construction example

**Problem:** The owner prepares 20 routine quote follow-ups each week. Each currently takes eight minutes. A reusable template with AI-assisted drafting is estimated to reduce preparation to three minutes including review.

**Capacity released:** 20 × (8 − 3) = 100 minutes/week, approximately 1.67 hours. At an assumed $45/hour, that is $75/week of staff capacity, or $300 over four weeks. It is not automatically $300 in cash savings.

**First action:** Teach the owner to use an approved follow-up template. The owner supplies the agreed price and checks job details. No AI measurement or automatic pricing claim.

**Draft output:** “Hi [customer], just checking whether you have any questions about the quote for [job]. Before we confirm the schedule, please let us know [missing detail]. Thanks, [name].”

**Business case:** Include training/setup fees, software, review and maintenance costs. Do not count both released staff hours and the revenue those same hours might generate without explaining the overlap. Track actual preparation time and corrections for two weeks before deciding on an automation.
