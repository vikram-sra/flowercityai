The task

The owner rewrites 20 routine quote follow-ups each week. Each takes an estimated eight minutes. Prices and job details are already agreed; the repeated writing is the problem.

Our recommendation

Start with a reusable template and practical training. Use approved quote details to prepare a draft. The owner checks the job, price and message before sending. A new automation may not be necessary.

What the draft could look like

Hi Sam, just checking whether you have any questions about your driveway quote. Before we confirm the schedule, could you let us know a good time to discuss the next step? Thanks, Alex.

Your next 30 days

  1. Week 1: Check the baseline and prepare one approved template.
  2. Week 2: Practise with fictional or redacted examples. Check every draft.
  3. Week 3: Record actual time and corrections for a small set of follow-ups.
  4. Week 4: Keep, improve or stop. Consider automation only if the evidence supports it.

Keep people in control

The owner approves pricing, measurements, scheduling and external messages. Use only information authorized for the chosen tool. Do not upload customer details into an AI account without checking the arrangement.
