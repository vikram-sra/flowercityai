How payment works

You invoice for real services. The eligible customer follows their funding agreement and may need to pay before reimbursement. Quote the full price. Do not count grants twice, add percentages, conceal rebates or treat the client’s grant as your extra revenue.

Submit your actual course outline, delivery format, instructor experience and vendor relationships for review. Ask what qualifies substantively. Do not rename an ineligible workshop to bypass the rule.
