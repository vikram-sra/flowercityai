Small things that lift replies

  • Send from a real name, not "Flower City AI" — people reply to people.
  • Keep the subject short and specific. "Quick reply from Flower City AI" beats "Thank you for your inquiry."
  • Never lead with price. Lead with their problem and the grant. Price is a call conversation.
  • Two follow-ups, then rest. Most replies come on the 2nd touch — but don't nag past that.
  • Text + email the same day if you have both. Different people check different inboxes.
  • Log every lead in your tracker with the date you replied, so nothing goes cold.
