What to sell first

Sell what's easy to deliver and easy to fund — then climb.

You don't have to offer everything on day one. Lead with the products that carry low delivery risk and the clearest grant path, bank the proof and the cash, then move up to the higher-value implementation work.

Rule of thumb: consultation first, training next, deployment later. You can't do direct deployments solo yet — so those are Phase 2, not your opening offer.

Phase 1 · Offer now

Advisory & Teaching

Nothing to deploy or maintain. You advise and you teach — near-zero technical risk, fast value, and the natural on-ramp to grant funding.

Consultation — AI Readiness Audit + Roadmap

$500–$1,500

Review a client's workflows, tools and pain points, then hand them a plain-English roadmap of what to automate and what it's worth. This is the Digital Modernization & Adoption Plan (DMAP).

EffortLow–Med GrantDMAP (planning)
Why it's first: every client relationship starts here — it's advisory work you write, not deploy, and it's the exact document grants are awarded against, so it unlocks funding for everything after it (including the training).

AI Team Training

$500–$2,000

Teach a team to actually use ChatGPT / Gemini / Copilot for their daily work — writing, email, research, summarizing. On-site or virtual workshops plus a simple resource guide.

EffortLow GrantCOJG · up to ~$15K/employee*
Why it's second: the consultation tells you exactly what to train on — and it's teaching, not building: nothing breaks, nothing to host. *COJG has covered $10K–$15K per employee (50%–83%) when intakes are open — paused in 2026, verify before pitching; training can also sit inside a DMAP.
Phase 2 · Add as you grow

Implementation & Recurring

Higher effort and skill. Grow into these — or bring in a contractor — once Phase 1 has given you proof, cash, and a written DMAP to fund the build.

Workflow Automation

$1,000–$5,000

EffortMedium GrantTDP (implementation)
Bridge product: your first step from advice into building. Low-risk automations are learnable solo; scope tightly.

Customer-Service AI / Chatbots

Deploy chatbots on a website, WhatsApp, or social to answer FAQs and book appointments 24/7. This is the hands-on deployment work you can't do solo today.

EffortHigh GrantTDP (implementation)
Deliver via a contractor first — or after you've upskilled. Sell it once you can stand behind the deployment; don't lead with it.

Monthly Retainer

$300–$800/mo

Ongoing maintenance, updates, and priority support for whatever you've built. Your recurring, compounding revenue base.

EffortLow–Med (ongoing) GrantNot grant-funded
Only after a delivery: you need something live to maintain. Offer it at the end of every Phase 2 project.

How to climb the ladder

  • Phase 1 pays for Phase 2. Training + audit revenue and real case studies give you the cash and credibility to take on bigger builds.
  • Every audit is a DMAP. The roadmaps you write in Phase 1 are the documents that unlock TDP implementation funding — so Phase 1 literally seeds Phase 2 deals.
  • Hire the deployment, don't fake it. When a client needs a chatbot before you can build it solo, subcontract a specialist (your business plan's first scaling trigger) and keep the client relationship.
  • End every project with a retainer. Recurring revenue is what turns one-off projects into a business.

Prices reflect the ranges in your business plan; grant names, amounts, and eligibility change often and are summarized from mid-2026 research — confirm current intake with each program before committing to a client. This is a planning tool, not legal, tax, or funding advice.
