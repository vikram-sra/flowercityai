Delivery boundaries

Software subscriptions, extra integrations, ongoing support and grant applications are separate. Funding screening may be included in a fit conversation; any detailed application work is scoped independently. Do not promise autonomous estimating, voice agents, complex bespoke software or unrestricted support. The customer owns their plan and accounts.

Watch your time

At an assumed value of $75/hour for your time, a $500 audit taking eight hours plus $50 in cash costs loses $150 before overhead. Time-box delivery and measure it. Do not make every audit a loss-leader for a future build.

Funding is a separate decision

A commercial audit is not automatically an approved DMAP. Funded training needs eligibility confirmation. An OCI-funded DMAP consultant cannot assume they can also deliver the same customer’s funded technology demonstration. Check the register before offering funded work.

Completion evidence

  • Audit: a useful plan the client can act on without hiring you again.
  • Training: employees demonstrate a task and identify errors.
  • Automation: normal, duplicate and failure cases work; the client can pause it.
